# Sources and their limits

Verification levels used in the rule registry: **TEXT_READ**, **VIA_SUMMARY**, **SECONDARY**, **UNVERIFIED — source verification required**.

| Source | Used for | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TKBB Participation Finance Standards No. 5, *Mudarabah* — https://tkbb.org.tr/upload/Mudarabah%20Standard_ENG.pdf | Mudarabah rules (§2.2.2, §2.2.4, §2.4.1, §2.4.5–2.4.7, §2.5, §2.6.4) | TEXT_READ | Turkish participation-banking standard (a recognised institutional standard-setter), **not AAOIFI**; Turkish jurisdiction. Its footnotes cite IIFA decisions. |
| TKBB Participation Finance Standards No. 7, *Musharakah* (23.06.2025) — https://ar.tkbb.org.tr/upload/4630513561-ingilizce-musareke.pdf | Musharakah rules (Art. 11, 12, 14, 15–17, 19–21, 25 and justifications) | TEXT_READ | Same caveats. |
| IIFA Resolution 30 (5/4) *Muqaradah and Investment Certificates* — https://iifa-aifi.org/en/32300.html | No manager guarantee of capital/fixed profit | VIA_SUMMARY | Page fetched through a summariser; confirm clause numbers against the official edition. |
| IIFA Resolutions 40-41 (2/5, 3/5) *Keeping a Promise and Murabahah to the Purchase Orderer* — https://iifa-aifi.org/en/32332.html | Possession and risk before the sale; promises | VIA_SUMMARY | As above. The task brief's paraphrase of this resolution was cross-checked against it. |
| State Bank of Pakistan, *Compendium of Shariah Standards* (to 31 Jul 2025) — https://www.sbp.org.pk/assets/document/publications/Compendium.pdf | Cites AAOIFI SS8, SS12, SS13, SS23, SS40, SS46 clause numbers through amendments and footnotes | SECONDARY | A regulator's amendments to AAOIFI texts, **Pakistan** jurisdiction. It confirms the clause exists and what the regulator changed; it does not reproduce the base clause text. SS13 was adopted "without amendment". |
| AAOIFI Shari'ah Standards SS8, SS12, SS13, SS18, SS23, SS38, SS40, SS45, SS46, SS47, SS49 | Primary technical benchmark | **UNVERIFIED** | Full text is members-only; the mirrors tried were blocked or captcha-protected. **No clause content is asserted beyond what is listed above.** |
| AAOIFI FAS 51 *Participatory Ventures* (announcement, 10 Nov 2025) | Accounting benchmark (replaces FAS 3 / FAS 4 for its scope) | UNVERIFIED | Announcement existence only; text not read. |
| Bangladesh Bank / Bangladeshi Shariah council rules | — | **Not researched** | Required before launch. |

No quotation in this repository is presented as exact unless it was read in the source text; everything else is a paraphrase.
